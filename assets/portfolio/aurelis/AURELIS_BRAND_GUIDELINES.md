## 2. BRAND POSITIONING

AURELIS represents the pinnacle of **Modern Luxury Fragrance**. It departs from the loud, ornate tropes of conventional fragrance marketing to embody a restrained contemporary ethos:

* **Quiet Confidence**: Luxury communicates through silence, negative space, and material weight rather than ostentatious embellishments or excessive gold.
* **Sensory Primacy**: The visual and spatial language triggers tactile imagination—smoked crystal, chilled brass, warm amber resonance, fine cotton paper.
* **Gender-Neutral Sophistication**: Crafted with architectural geometry and timeless poise, free from gendered stereotypes.
* **Architectural Minimalism**: Every edge, chamfer, letterform, and millimeter of whitespace serves a defined visual purpose.

### 4.2 Wordmark Typographic Design
* **Primary Letterforms**: Bespoke high-contrast serif typography inspired by classical Didot and Cinzel.
* **Tracking**: Ultra-wide letter-spacing (`0.28em` / `letter-spacing: 0.28em`).
* **Accent Divider**: Fine horizontal hairline rules (`#CBB99F`) anchored by a central 45° faceted diamond pip representing a crystal scent droplet.
* **Working Descriptor**: `LUXURY FRAGRANCE` set in clean modern sans-serif at `0.52em` letter-spacing.

### 4.3 Monogram Architecture
* High-contrast architectural 'A' formed by a heavy serif pillar on the left, an ethereal hairline stroke on the right, and a suspended central diamond crystal prism.
* Encased within subtle dual concentric orbital rings for wax sealing, debossing, and app icon avatars.

### 4.4 Logo Usage Rules
1. **Clear Space**: Maintain a minimum exclusion zone equal to the height of the letter "A" on all four sides.
2. **Minimum Digital Size**:
   * Wordmark: Minimum width `140px` (screens) / `35mm` (print).
   * Monogram: Minimum width `24px` (digital) / `8mm` (packaging).
3. **Background Alignment**:
   * Use `logo-primary-light.svg` on Obsidian (`#0B0B0C`) and Deep Charcoal (`#1A1918`).
   * Use `logo-primary-dark.svg` on Warm Ivory (`#F7F5F0`) and neutral stone.
4. **Incorrect Usages**:
   * Never condense, stretch, or alter the kerning of the wordmark.
   * Never apply bright rainbow, neon, or 3D bevel layer effects.
   * Never enclose the wordmark in decorative crests, shields, leaves, or crowns.

* **Flacon Silhouette**: Monolithic rectangular geometry with crisp, micro-chamfered corners.
* **Glass Treatment**: Outer layer smoked obsidian fade; thick transparent glass floor (70mm solid crystal foundation) giving tremendous physical weight and optical gravity.
* **Inner Liquid Core**: Rich, translucent cognac/amber resonance (`#5A3C20`) that catches backlight and glows warmly from within the smoked glass.
* **Cap Architecture**: Solid brushed champagne brass cylinder (`#CBB99F`) with radial micro-grain finish, engraved monogram on the apex, and a satisfying dual-action magnetic closure.
* **Label Specifications**:
  * Material: 320gsm unbleached warm ivory fine cotton rag paper.
  * Size: Centered on the lower-middle third of the bottle face.
  * Printing: Blind letterpress debossing of the AURELIS wordmark, crisp charcoal typography for NOIR ÉCLAT, and a delicate hot-stamped champagne hairline divider.

## 8. ART DIRECTION & PHOTOGRAPHY PROTOCOL

### 8.1 The Eight Creative Pillars
1. **DARKNESS**: Enveloping chiaroscuro. The frame is anchored by deep obsidian voids (`#0B0B0C`). At least 50% of the frame is allowed to fall into velvety shadow.
2. **LIGHT**: Directional and intentional. Thin, warm shafts of champagne light cutting diagonally across the scene like sunlight piercing a museum archive.
3. **GLASS**: Prismatic refraction. Heavy lead-free flint crystal scattering warm golden caustic patterns across dark ground surfaces.
4. **SHADOW**: Soft penumbras with controlled edge falloff. Never harsh flash; always sculpted chiaroscuro.
5. **REFLECTION**: Mirror-like stillness on polished black granite or basalt plinths. Never cheap acrylic or high-gloss plastic.
6. **TEXTURE**: Tactile juxtaposition of cold brushed brass, chilled crystal glass, rough volcanic stone, and warm fibrous cotton paper.
7. **ATMOSPHERE**: Suspended micro-particulates and subtle atmospheric haze catching a low-angle rim beam.
8. **SPACE**: Generous negative space (40%+ minimum) providing room for editorial breathing and high-fashion composure.

### 8.2 Studio Lighting Setup (3-Point Chiaroscuro)
* **Key Light**: 45° high-side placement using a honeycomb grid softbox fitted with a warm 3200K gel, casting a directional beam across the bottle shoulder and label.
* **Rim Light**: 1x4 strip softbox positioned behind and slightly to the right, tracing the edge of the glass flacon and separating the obsidian bottle from the dark background.
* **Negative Fill**: Obsidian black foamcore flags on the unlit flank to deepen shadow density and eliminate stray ambient scatter.

## 10. SOCIAL MEDIA VISUAL RULES (STAGE 2/3 BLUEPRINT)

When creating the 5 upcoming social creatives in future stages, adhere strictly to these constraints:

1. **Aspect Ratios**:
   * Feed Posts / Carousel: `4:5` (1080 x 1350 px).
   * Stories / Reels / Teasers: `9:16` (1080 x 1920 px).
2. **Composition & Grid**:
   * Minimum `80px` internal padding from screen edges.
   * Central monolithic product placement or golden-ratio offset (bottle on right third, headline on left third).
3. **Typography on Social**:
   * Display headlines always set in Didot uppercase with `0.15em` tracking.
   * Captions and metadata in Helvetica Neue / Inter at `0.25em` tracking.
4. **Palette Consistency**:
   * Posts must never introduce colors outside the core palette (Obsidian, Warm Ivory, Champagne, Stone, Charcoal).

## 11. AI COMMERCIAL & MOTION DIRECTION (STAGE 3 BLUEPRINT)

When producing the 10-15s AI commercial reel in Stage 3:

* **Pacing**: Hypnotic, contemplative, slow-motion (60fps to 120fps interpolated). Zero quick cuts or jarring whip-pans.
* **Camera Movements**:
  1. *Shot 1 (Opening Macro)*: Extreme close-up sliding across the brushed brass cap with light glinting off the engraved monogram.
  2. *Shot 2 (Liquid & Glass)*: Light beam slowly penetrating the smoked glass, igniting the amber liquid into radiant cognac warmth.
  3. *Shot 3 (Reveal)*: Slow vertical tilt down from the cap to the embossed label resting on the black granite plinth with caustics.
  4. *Shot 4 (Hero Lockup)*: Complete product silhouette centered in vast negative space with the animated typography "AURELIS / THE ART OF SCENT".
* **Sonic Direction**: Resonant sub-bass drone, crisp crystal resonance, subtle atmospheric air tone, whispered editorial voiceover.
