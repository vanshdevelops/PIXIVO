## 1. CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE & NARRATIVE PROGRESSION

The AURELIS social campaign is engineered as a **deliberate five-stage narrative arc**. Rather than producing disconnected, repetitive product advertisements, the sequence unfolds like a high-fashion editorial campaign:

```text
┌──────────────────────────┐
│  01. BRAND INTRODUCTION  │ -> Establishes the AURELIS luxury world, silence, and ethos
└────────────┬─────────────┘
             │
┌────────────▼─────────────┐
│    02. PRODUCT REVEAL    │ -> Unveils NOIR ÉCLAT in pure monolithic front elevation
└────────────┬─────────────┘
             │
┌────────────▼─────────────┐
│   03. FRAGRANCE NOTES    │ -> Deconstructs the 3-tier olfactory pyramid in architectural format
└────────────┬─────────────┘
             │
┌────────────▼─────────────┐
│    04. CAMPAIGN MOOD     │ -> Ignites emotional desire through low-key chiaroscuro & mystery
└────────────┬─────────────┘
             │
┌────────────▼─────────────┐
│    05. LAUNCH CREATIVE   │ -> Final conversion-oriented key visual with restrained CTA
└──────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. DETAILED CREATIVE SPECIFICATIONS

### Creative 01 — Brand Introduction
* **Filename**: `aurelis-social-01-brand-introduction-4x5.webp` (`1080×1350`) / `-1x1.webp` (`1080×1080`)
* **Role**: Atmospheric brand introduction. Establishes presence before commercial intent.
* **Core Copy**: 
  - `AURELIS`
  - `THE ART OF SCENT`
  - `"Fragrance, composed as an experience."`
  - `HAUTE PARFUMERIE · PARIS`
* **Visual Direction**: Centered architectural monogram medallion, deep obsidian radial depth, dual concentric hairline framing, generous negative space.
* **Concept Disclosure**: `SELF-INITIATED CONCEPT · PIXIVO ARCHIVE`

### Creative 02 — Product Reveal
* **Filename**: `aurelis-social-02-product-reveal-4x5.webp` (`1080×1350`) / `-1x1.webp` (`1080×1080`)
* **Role**: Definitive first presentation of the locked canonical NOIR ÉCLAT bottle.
* **Core Copy**:
  - `AURELIS`
  - `THE FLAGSHIP FRAGRANCE`
  - `NOIR ÉCLAT`
  - `EAU DE PARFUM · 100 ML · PARIS`
  - `Smoked Obsidian Glass · Cognac Amber Core · Brushed Champagne Brass`
* **Visual Direction**: Canonical bottle centered on black granite pedestal with subtle caustic reflections, warm amber core backlight, pristine Didot typography.

### Creative 03 — Fragrance Notes
* **Filename**: `aurelis-social-03-fragrance-notes-4x5.webp` (`1080×1350`) / `-1x1.webp` (`1080×1080`)
* **Role**: Editorial deconstruction of the olfactory composition.
* **Core Copy**:
  - `01 / TOP NOTES`: Calabrian Bergamot · Madagascar Pink Pepper (*Immediate cold citrus illumination*)
  - `02 / HEART NOTES`: Florentine Iris Concrete · Mineral Violet Leaf (*Powdery couture elegance*)
  - `03 / BASE NOTES`: Smoked Haitian Vetiver · Atlas Cedar · Golden Amber (*Enduring subterranean sillage*)
* **Visual Direction**: Three floating architectural cards in dark charcoal with subtle amber glowing accents on left margins, restrained Didot and Helvetica typography.

### Creative 04 — Campaign / Mood Creative
* **Filename**: `aurelis-social-04-campaign-mood-4x5.webp` (`1080×1350`) / `-1x1.webp` (`1080×1080`)
* **Role**: Emotional resonance, tactile sensuality, and brand mystery.
* **Core Copy**:
  - `"Leave an impression without saying a word."`
  - `NOIR ÉCLAT · THE ART OF SCENT`
* **Visual Direction**: Extreme chiaroscuro. The bottle emerges from pitch obsidian shadow, sculpted by a razor-thin 3200K champagne rim light tracing the silhouette. High-contrast negative fill on the right flank.

### Creative 05 — Launch Creative
* **Filename**: `aurelis-social-05-launch-4x5.webp` (`1080×1350`) / `-1x1.webp` (`1080×1080`)
* **Role**: Flagship launch key visual combining product elevation, amber caustics, and restrained conversion poise.
* **Core Copy**:
  - `AURELIS / THE ART OF SCENT`
  - `NOIR ÉCLAT`
  - `EAU DE PARFUM · 100 ML`
  - CTA Button: `EXPLORE THE COLLECTION`
  - Footer: `PARIS · CONCEPT PRESENTATION`
* **Visual Direction**: Symmetrical luxury composition with gold-standard caustic radiance, champagne hairline divider, and quiet luxury CTA pill.

## 4. PRODUCT CONSISTENCY & IMMUTABLE RULES
1. **Single Product Source of Truth**: All bottle visuals strictly inherit the locked Stage 3.1 proportions, cap geometry, and label debossing.
2. **Zero Fake Metrics**: No claims of impressions, conversion rates, follower growth, or fictional retail availability.
3. **Palette Purity**: Muted warm Champagne (`#CBB99F`), Obsidian (`#0B0B0C`), Deep Charcoal (`#1A1918`), and Warm Ivory (`#F7F5F0`). Never saturated yellow gold.
