## 1. Executive Summary & Narrative Arc

The AURELIS commercial film is an exercise in quiet confidence and sensory restraint. The narrative does not rely on spoken dialogue, quick cuts, or bombastic visual effects. Instead, it lets **light, shadow, physical materiality, and acoustic resonance** establish an atmosphere of undeniable luxury.

The progression moves through four distinct states of presence:
$$\text{DARKNESS (Anticipation)} \longrightarrow \text{REVEAL (Emergence)} \longrightarrow \text{HERO (Form \& Optics)} \longrightarrow \text{IDENTITY (Permanence)}$$

## 2. Shot-by-Shot Production Blueprint

### SHOT 01 — THE DARKNESS
- **Timecode:** `00:00.000 — 00:03.000` (Duration: 3.0 sec)
- **Narrative Purpose:** Establish atmospheric tension, space, and materiality before the product is revealed.
- **Environment:** Monolithic dark architectural studio plinth carved from honed black granite and smoked obsidian glass.
- **Camera Direction:** 
  - Focal Length: 85mm T1.5 prime lens.
  - Movement: Extremely slow, controlled macro push-in along the horizontal plane. Zero camera shake.
  - Depth of Field: Razor-shallow ($f/2.0$), soft falloff into deep velvety shadow.
- **Lighting Direction:**
  - Directional champagne key light skimming the background plinth edge at a 30° angle.
  - Deep obsidian shadow gradient ($90\%$ of frame held in pure dark value).
- **Master Still References:**
  - Vertical (9:16): `assets/video/shots/shot-01/aurelis-shot-01-darkness-9x16.webp`
  - Widescreen (16:9): `assets/video/shots/shot-01/aurelis-shot-01-darkness-16x9.webp`
  - Portrait (4:5): `assets/video/shots/shot-01/aurelis-shot-01-darkness-4x5.webp`
- **Production AI Video Prompt (Image-to-Video / Gen-3 / Sora):**
  > *"Cinematic high-end luxury fragrance commercial shot in near darkness. Architectural smoked glass plinth and honed obsidian stone surfaces. A controlled, ultra-narrow warm champagne rim light slowly grazes across a precision dark background. Slow, deliberate camera dolly-in, 85mm anamorphic macro cinematography, photorealistic physical materials, soft chiaroscuro falloff, meditative stillness, quiet luxury, 24fps motion blur, zero distortion, no particles, no smoke explosions."*
