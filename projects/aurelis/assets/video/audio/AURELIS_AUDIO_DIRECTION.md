## 1. Acoustic Philosophy

A luxury fragrance cannot be felt through aggressive sound. The sound design of NOIR ÉCLAT must evoke the **weight of crystal glass, the cooling temperature of honed stone, and the warm tactile presence of amber liquid**.

The audio operates across three distinct sensory layers:
1. **The Sub-Atmosphere (Bed):** Deep, resonant room drone that makes the speakers disappear into darkness.
2. **The Tactile Foley (Micro-Texture):** Ultra-high fidelity recordings of heavy glass friction, magnetic cap click, and delicate liquid viscosity.
3. **The Tonal Harmonic (Swell):** A quiet, warm cello/bowed metallic harmonic that blooms as the product is revealed.

## 3. Mix & Mastering Standards

- **Frequency Allocation:**
  - `20 Hz – 60 Hz`: Low-frequency rumble & weight (subtle, controlled, zero distortion).
  - `250 Hz – 500 Hz`: Warm wood & cello body warmth.
  - `2 kHz – 5 kHz`: Tactile edge definition (magnetic cap engagement).
  - `10 kHz – 20 kHz`: Air and spatial room reverb tail.
- **Dynamic Range:** Uncompressed micro-dynamics to preserve live-recorded acoustic textures.
- **Music Restrictions:** Strictly NO drums, NO trap beats, NO corporate acoustic guitar, NO aggressive horns. Silence is luxury.
