01 / BRAND INTRODUCTION

MORE THAN A FRAGRANCE.

AURELIS explores the relationship between scent, atmosphere and memory — creating fragrances designed to become part of the moments that define us.

Departing from the loud, ornate tropes of conventional fragrance houses, AURELIS embraces architectural minimalism, quiet confidence, and tactile sensory primacy. We compose in deep shadows and luminous caustics—where monolithic obsidian glass cradles an amber heart, and warm champagne brass accents meet tactile cotton rag.

01 Quiet Confidence

Luxury communicating through silence and material weight rather than ostentation.

02 Sensory Primacy

Chilled crystal, warm cognac resonance, and tactile cotton letterpress design.

03 Architectural Poise

Monolithic rectangular geometry anchored by a weighted solid glass foundation.

03 / OLFACTORY PYRAMID

THE ARCHITECTURE OF SCENT

A layered composition structured to unfold seamlessly across hours.

IMMEDIATE IMPRESSION · FIRST 20 MINUTES

Bergamot · Pink Pepper

Bright, luminous, piquantly aromatic

A razor-sharp beam of cold Calabrian bergamot zest pierces through dark ambient shadow, energized by the crushed warmth of Madagascar pink pepper berries. It establishes immediate presence without aggression.

Citrus Luminescence Cracked Pepper Airy Crispness
SENSORY CORE · 2 TO 6 HOURS

Iris · Violet Leaf

Powdery floral elegance with mineral verdancy

As the opening brightness recedes, a velvety heart of Florentine orris concrete emerges. Combined with the dewy, mineral verdancy of violet leaf, it imparts an unmistakable haute-couture texture akin to soft suede.

Florentine Orris Mineral Leaf Powdery Sophistication
ENDURING SILLAGE · 8 TO 16 HOURS

Vetiver · Cedar · Amber

Deep, smoky, resinous warmth with commanding sillage

The anchor of the composition. Haitian smoked vetiver and Atlas cedarwood weave into a dense tapestry of fossilized golden amber. The drydown lingers on skin and textiles as an indelible personal memory.

Smoked Vetiver Atlas Cedar Warm Cognac Amber
TOP Bergamot & Pink Pepper Light Pierce
HEART Iris & Violet Leaf Textural Suede
BASE Vetiver, Cedar & Amber Resinous Depth
04 / CREATIVE NARRATIVE

COMPOSED IN CONTRAST.

NOIR ÉCLAT is born from the tension between radiant illumination and dense obsidian darkness.

BRIGHTNESS & DEPTH

Calabrian bergamot zest and pink pepper bring vivid crystalline light, immediately counterbalanced by the subterranean gravity of smoked Haitian vetiver.

FRESHNESS & WARMTH

The crisp mineral coolness of violet leaves softens into a smoldering foundation of amber, cognac tones, and dry Atlas cedarwood.

PRESENCE & INTIMACY

Formulated with an intentional projection curve—commanding respect in open spaces while inviting profound intimacy at close proximity.
